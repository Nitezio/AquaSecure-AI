Problem Statement

Water-treatment operations depend on networked sensors, actuators, and PLCs. However, securing them remains a critical challenge.

Critical Infrastructure Risk

Manipulated equipment can affect flow, tank levels, and chemical processes, endangering water supply.

Detection Limitation

Static threshold alarms fail to capture coordinated or gradual attacks, and conventional alerts provide limited explanations.

Operator Decision Gap

Smaller utilities lack dedicated OT-security teams. Operators need rapid, understandable evidence during an incident.

3. Explain

AquaSecure AI does not only identify abnormal behaviour—it tells operators what changed, why it is suspicious and where to investigate.

Dynamic Learning

Hybrid Detection

Lightweight & Accessible

Project Impacts

Operational Impact

  • Faster visibility of abnormal process behaviour.
  • Centralized telemetry and threat monitoring.
  • Clearer operator decisions during incidents.

Social Impact

  • Supports the reliability of essential water services.
  • Helps protect public confidence in water-treatment infrastructure.

Technical Impact

  • Demonstrates explainable & physics-aware OT monitoring.
  • Connects AI inference directly to real-time visualization.

Accessibility Impact

  • Makes complex model output understandable to non-ML operators.
  • Explores lightweight monitoring for resource-constrained utilities.

Future Improvement

A realistic development roadmap for advancing the prototype.

Near-Term Technical Goals

  • Fine-grained attack-type classification.
  • Incorporation of additional physics invariants.
  • Adversarial robustness testing against evasion.
  • Testing on BATADAL and other ICS datasets.
  • Broader SWaT sensor coverage.

Long-Term Deployment

  • Slow sensor-drift detection capabilities.
  • Edge or TinyML deployment for local monitoring.
  • Operator-feedback studies for UI refinement.
  • Improved alert prioritization logic.
  • Secure real-facility pilots after safety validation.
